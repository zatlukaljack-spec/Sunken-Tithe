The Sunken Tithe
DM Reference  ·  D&D 5e  ·  Levels 2–3  ·  3–4 hrs  ·  4 players
Read aloud to players
"The well of Bormir has been dry for three days, and the villagers are desperate. Where water once flowed, a crumbling stone staircase now descends into darkness — slick with algae, smelling of old rain and something older still. The village elder presses a rusted iron key into your hands and whispers: 'Whatever the temple wants, give it back.'"
What's really going on

Dag Ironboot cracked the well wall with shoddy repairs, draining water into the ancient temple below and destabilizing a magical seal. Marta's late husband's gold idol now jams the valve. The temple predates Brother Coyne's religion entirely — and something inside is watching.

Session goal

Descend the dry well, navigate the drowned temple, unblock the valve (returning the idol), and optionally appease — or defeat — the Keeper of the Tithe in the Inner Sanctum.

1
The Muddy Descent Athletics DC 12
Atmosphere

Wet earth and copper smell. Dripping water echoes. Torchlight barely reaches the bottom.

Challenge

Algae-slick stairs — DC 12 Athletics or Acrobatics or slide down, arriving prone (1d4 bludgeoning). A rope anchored above grants advantage.

Combat

Two Giant Frogs in the flooded landing pool. They prioritize prone targets for Swallow.

2
Hall of Offerings Puzzle + Combat
Atmosphere

Carved stone shelves with clay pots and corroded coins. A low hum — water vibrating through hidden pipes, almost like chanting.

Pressure plate floor

Land animal tiles = dart trap. DC 13 Perception to spot. DC 14 Dex save or 2d6 piercing. Water creature tiles are safe. DC 14 Religion/History to identify.

Combat

Three Mud Mephits rise from collapsed floor sections as ancient guardians.

Skip tip: If running short on time, describe this room as "already disturbed — pots shattered, plates triggered long ago." Jump straight to Room 3.
3
The Clogged Valve Time Pressure!
Atmosphere

Pressurized silence — the feeling of something about to burst. Air tastes metallic.

The Valve Puzzle

Marta's idol jams the valve. DC 15 Strength brute force OR DC 13 Investigation to find the latch. Marta's map = auto-success. Forcing it without removing the idol: 2d6 bludgeoning, all prone.

Time Pressure — 3 Rounds!

Ceiling cracks. After 3 rounds: partial collapse deals 3d6 damage. DC 14 Dex save for half. No enemies — let the environment be the threat.

4
The Inner Sanctum Boss Room
Atmosphere

Eerie stillness. Domed, bone-dry chamber. A stone idol of a bloated frog-like deity on a throne of river rock. Its polished-stone eyes seem to follow the party.

Inscription
"Return what was taken freely, or be taken in return."
Resolution

Place a personal or magical item in the offering bowl → spring is restored. Coins alone won't work.

If they refuse or disrespect

The Keeper of the Tithe awakens. See Combat tab.

The Tithe Idol
Worth 75 gp to a collector. But Marta will cry if they sell it. Returning it unlocks Marta's reward.
Reward for return: Her husband's journal + crude map to a nearby ruin. Great next-session hook.
Keeper's Eye
Polished river-stone eye from the throne. Arcane Focus for druids or clerics.
Property
1/long rest: Cast Shape Water (cantrip) for free. Always warm and slightly damp.
The Offering Bowl
Ancient engraved bronze bowl. Any liquid poured in is purified (removes poison and disease) and faintly blessed. Holds 1 pint.
Fragile: any damage cracks it and removes the power. Weighs 2 lbs.
Players stuck on a puzzle?
  • Have Dag Ironboot's voice echo from above: "Did ye find the latch yet?!"
  • Still stuck? A wandering Giant Frog bursts through a wall, shatters a clue into plain sight, and makes the solution obvious through chaos. Use destruction as a hint machine.
Running out of time?
  • Skip Room 2 (Hall of Offerings) entirely — describe it as "already disturbed, pots shattered, plates triggered long ago." Jump straight to the Valve.
  • Cut the Keeper's HP to 40 and remove Tidal Surge. The story still lands — just tighter.
Party bored or disengaged?
  • Drop in Pell Quickwick — a halfling explorer hiding on a ledge in the Hall of Offerings, starving, with one useful clue and one wild theory. Instant energy. Let him be funny, panicked, or both.
